# آخرین پناهگاه 3D — Last Shelter Tactical FPS

A mobile-first, browser-based 3D first-person survival shooter prototype using Three.js.

## Play
https://mahdimokhtari39161-svg.github.io/lifeos-app/survival-game/

## Controls
- **Mobile:** left touch joystick to move; drag the right side of the screen to look; FIRE to shoot; aim button to zoom; reload and sprint buttons.
- **Desktop:** WASD to move, mouse to look, left-click to fire, R to reload, Shift to sprint.

## Included
- 3D outdoor combat zone with a fortified compound, cover objects, terrain, trees, fog, and directional lighting
- Procedurally assembled humanoid enemy soldiers with simple movement animation
- First-person rifle model, crosshair, aim zoom, muzzle flash, tracer, hit marker, ammo and reload
- Enemy waves, player health, kill count, pause, and local progress save
- Responsive touch interface and installable PWA shell

## Important
This is an original playable 3D prototype inspired by tactical first-person shooters. It is **not Call of Duty**, does not use Call of Duty assets, and is not yet a full AAA production. Character models, animations, sound design, maps, multiplayer, and performance optimization need further production work. Three.js is loaded from jsDelivr, so an internet connection is required on first load.
